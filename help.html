Quick answers

What is my Wallet Password?

After you sign in with the User ID and sign-in passkey the admin gave you, the dashboard asks you to create your own Wallet Password. It locks your wallet, only you know it, and you enter it each time you open your wallet. Nobody else can open your wallet, not even the admin.

I forgot my Wallet Password.

It cannot be recovered, because it is the only key to your wallet. On the unlock window choose Forgot your Wallet Password? to erase the wallet and start again with a new one. Everything saved in it is deleted, so keep an exported backup.

I forgot my sign-in passkey.

Ask the admin (angel) to give you a new one under Security → Manage Users. Your wallet is not affected.

Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

Your wallet is encrypted in your browser with a key made from your Wallet Password, then stored as unreadable data. Only you can open it. Other users, the admin and whoever runs the database cannot read it.

Can I create my own account?

No. Only the admin creates User IDs. Ask the admin for one.

How do I back up or move my passwords?

Go to Vault → Export to download a JSON or CSV file, or use Security → Data → Export. To bring passwords in, use Vault → Import.

I deleted a password by mistake.

Open the Recycle Bin and press Restore. Deleted passwords wait there for 30 days before they are removed for good.

How do I change my passwords or add a user?

Open Security. Use Change Wallet Password for your wallet, and Manage Users to change your sign-in passkey. Only the admin (angel) can add or remove users.

Why did my wallet lock on its own?

The auto-lock timer ran out. Enter your Wallet Password to open it again. You can make the timer shorter or longer on the Security page.
